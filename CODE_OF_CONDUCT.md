# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
`totm` (part of `tot`) a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

* Be kind, be direct, assume good intent.
* Give and accept constructive feedback.
* Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, learn.
* Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, publishing private info, or other conduct that
a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for violations.
